About Corelink

A trading partner you can reach and rely on.

Corelink Oversees was set up to make cross-border trade simpler for businesses that do not have a full logistics department.

Our story

Importers and exporters often lose time and money to unreliable suppliers, unclear costs and paperwork errors. We built Corelink Oversees to remove those problems.

We combine sourcing, inspection, documentation and freight coordination under one roof, and we report on every shipment in plain language.
